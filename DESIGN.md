## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies high-precision geotechnical engineering, structural stabilization, and evidence-based field geology. The design language must evoke deep structural reliability, engineering mastery, mathematical exactitude, and uncompromising safety. The target audience comprises chief engineers, industrial project managers, infrastructure developers, and technical directors who demand hard metrics, rigorous compliance, and technological superiority.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate Technical Modernism** with **Blueprint Brutalism**:
- **Blueprint Precision**: Architectural hairline grids, technical datum marks, structural dimension callouts, and clean schematic framing.
- **Evidence-Based Engineering**: Prominence of empirical data, geotechnical cross-sections, soil horizon stratigraphy, structural load tolerances, and crisp analytical badges.
- **Tactile Material Honesty**: Light concrete-toned canvases paired with heavy graphite-slate structural anchor blocks, energized by high-visibility engineering safety orange.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from industrial geotechnical environments—reinforced concrete, solid structural steel, deep subterranean soil layers, and high-visibility field safety markers.

- **Primary (`#0F1B2D`)**: Deep subterranean graphite-navy. Serves as the primary anchor for dominant structural headers, technical toolbars, data card headers, and dark inverted operational panels.
- **Secondary (`#EE7828`)**: Industrial safety orange, directly derived from the company identity. Used selectively for high-urgency CTAs, active status indicators, dimension markers, key performance metrics, and primary interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#1C2B3E`)**: Technical slate blue, used for elevated containers, interactive borders in dark contexts, subheaders, and secondary toolbars.
- **Neutral (`#F4F5F7`)**: Light concrete gray, forming the foundational architectural background, preventing clinical white glare and reflecting raw concrete materials.
- **Supportive Accents**:
  - `safety-orange-hover` (`#FF6A1A`): Luminous active interaction state for primary triggers.
  - `concrete-border` (`#D5D9DE`): High-precision 1px engineering outlines and card separators.
  - `concrete-subtle` (`#E8E9EA`): Recessed data cell fills and secondary module backgrounds.
  - `slate-muted` (`#627284`): Secondary technical body copy, unit annotations, and metadata labels.
  - `cad-grid` (`#E2E5E9`): Hairline coordinate grid overlays and chart division lines.

## Typography

The typographic system communicates structural permanence and technical rigor through a calibrated trio of functional typefaces:

1. **Display & Headlines (Manrope)**: Chosen for its geometric, heavy industrial cut, robust structural vertices, and outstanding legibility when set bold. It reflects structural beam geometry and architectural blueprints.
2. **Body & Interface (Inter)**: The standard for high-density information architecture. Provides clean neutral legibility across tabular metrics, geotechnical descriptions, specifications, and project narratives.
3. **Engineering & Metric Data (JetBrains Mono)**: Employed specifically for indexing values, step indicators (`[01]`, `[02]`), technical tolerances, coordinate values, load calculations, and badge metadata.

All numbers in data-heavy displays must use tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve vertical alignment across calculation sheets and tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an **Engineering Blueprint Grid** system:
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max container width of 1320px, 24px gutters, 40px outer safe margins. All components strictly snap to an 8px base rhythm (with a 4px fine cad-unit for borders, badges, and icon lockups).
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid scaling down to 768px with 16px gutters and 24px margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Single-column stack with 16px margins, prioritizing high-legibility metric chips and vertical process steps.

### Blueprint Framing & Axis Alignment
Sections utilize hairline dividers (`1px solid #D5D9DE`) that extend edge-to-edge. Major technical sections incorporate subtle technical coordinate datum marks at intersecting borders (such as tiny crosshairs `+` or section indices like `SEC_04 // GEOTECH`) in the outer margins to reinforce the engineering drawing aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft, decorative shadows. Instead, the design system implements **Structural Planar Tiers and Crisp Technical Outlines**:

- **Level 0 (Earth / Site Ground)**: Background surface in `#F4F5F7` or solid `#0F1B2D` for contrasting dark modules. May feature subtle SVG CAD dot-matrix or 24px hairline orthographic grid lines at 4% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Concrete Structural Plates / Cards)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` planes framed by crisp `1px solid #D5D9DE` outlines. No diffuse dropshadows; separation is strictly chromatic and volumetric.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Overlay Plates)**: A crisp, engineered hard offset shadow: `0px 4px 0px 0px #0F1B2D` or `0px 6px 16px -4px rgba(15, 27, 45, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Technical Inspection Flyouts)**: Elevated operational surfaces with `0px 16px 36px -8px rgba(15, 27, 45, 0.16)` bordered in `#1C2B3E`.
- **Accent Highlight Lines**: Cards may feature a top or left directional border strip in `2px solid #EE7828` to denote active, verified, or critical status.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Controlled Technical Micro-Radius** (`roundedness: 1` = 4px base radius):
- **Base Components (Cards, Panels, Inputs, Tables)**: `4px` radius (`rounded-sm`). This replicates machined industrial edges, milled steel, and precast concrete blocks, avoiding childish roundness while eliminating harsh unrefined corners.
- **Interactive Badges & Technical Pills**: Strict `2px` or `4px` chamfered feel. True capsule pills (`rounded-full`) are reserved solely for live telemetry status dots and micro-chips.
- **Blueprint Line Motifs**: 1px solid stroke styling across diagrams, dashed dimension tie-lines (`border-dashed`), and 45-degree corner notches for special technical callout brackets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Deep safety orange (`#EE7828`) background, `#FFFFFF` bold typography, 4px radius. Hover state shifts to `#FF6A1A` with an immediate 1px downward translation. Padding: `12px 24px` for standard, `16px 32px` for prominent inquiries. Accompanied by a technical arrow `→` icon.
- **Secondary (Structural)**: Dark graphite (`#0F1B2D`) fill with white text, ideal for high-contrast engineering actions and downloadable calculation reports.
- **Outline (Technical)**: Transparent background, `1px solid #0F1B2D` or `#D5D9DE`, dark slate text. On hover, background fills with `#E8E9EA`.

### Engineering Badges & Numerical Steps
- **Indexed Sequence Step (e.g., `01`, `02`, `03`)**: Large, low-opacity or highlighted numerals set in JetBrains Mono or heavy Manrope, positioned at card corners or as an orange accent tag (`#EE7828`).
- **Status Chips**: 4px radius, `4px 8px` padding, JetBrains Mono font (`11px`), subtle `#E8E9EA` background with a solid `#0F1B2D` border or safety-orange left dot. Used for geotechnical method codes (e.g., `[ВИЭР]`, `[ВЭЗ]`, `[ГОСТ 20522-2012]`).

### Cards & Data Modules
- **Evidence Metric Cards**: Pure white card with `1px solid #D5D9DE`, featuring large statistical readouts (e.g., `100+`, `15 000 км²`, `до 90%`) rendered in Manrope 800 at 44px with JetBrains Mono metric labels directly beneath.
- **Process Step Card**: Clean white plate with top border accent in `#EE7828` or `#0F1B2D`, structured sub-bullet checkpoints with orange checkmark ticks, and technical breadcrumbs.
- **Geotechnical Strata Preview Card**: High-contrast geological profile view with precise side-rail measurement guides, depth scale markings (0m to -50m), and callout pins.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #D5D9DE`, 4px radius, 14px Inter text. Focus state triggers `1px solid #0F1B2D` and a distinct outer outline `0 0 0 2px rgba(238, 120, 40, 0.2)`.
- **Labels**: High-contrast, uppercase 11px JetBrains Mono label above each input field with optional unit indicators (`[м]`, `[кПа]`, `[т/м³]`).

### Data Tables & Spec Sheets
- Alternating subtle rows in `#F4F5F7` and `#FFFFFF`.
- Headers set in dark graphite `#0F1B2D` with white or light gray text, aligned strictly with numeric tabular values right-aligned and categorical parameters left-aligned.
